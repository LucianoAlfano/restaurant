Before we talk

Questions, answered.

Are these real client projects?

No. All eight are fictional concepts with invented names, details, and prices.

What will my site cost?

You receive a written quote with the scope and ongoing costs before deciding.

Can I choose a theme?

Yes. A theme is a design starting point; your content, colors, images, and features make it yours.

Will I own the website?

Your proposal specifies the files and access included. The domain and service accounts should be in your name.

What can be automated?

Inquiry routing, reminders, spreadsheet or CRM updates, and other handoffs supported by your existing tools.

How long will it take?

Your proposal includes a timeline based on scope and when your content is ready.
